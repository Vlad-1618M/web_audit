What is Web Audit?

×

Web Audit looks at your public website the way a stranger on the internet would — and explains in plain language whether basic security hygiene looks okay.

It is not a full penetration test. It does not log into your site or change anything.

  • Read-only checks: headers, HTTPS, DNS email settings, exposed files
  • Reports saved on this Mac — not uploaded by default
  • Share the HTML report with your developer or host
